Heavenfall for Android: a modified version of 4u-native

Heavenfall is a modified version of 4u-native (MH4U for Android and PC), made by khorne-hub. Original project page:
https://github.com/khorne-hub/4u-native

This version is changed from the original: it has its own name, application id, icon, releases and signing key, and it
is not the official version of 4u-native. It is not made or endorsed by khorne-hub. The original's credits, its licence
(MH4U-GPL-3.0.txt) and its additional terms (MH4U-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.txt) are kept as they are.

This version's downloads and its complete source code are only at
https://github.com/popzol/heavenfall-recomp-android
